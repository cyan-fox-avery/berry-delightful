# Berry Delightful (working title)

A cozy, finite strawberry farming game — a small playable love letter to Fiona.

Restore a neglected patch of land into a beautiful working strawberry farm: plant → tend → harvest → sell or process → improve → unlock new varieties, methods, recipes, and possibilities. The season builds to the town's annual Strawberry Festival, where the restored farm opens to visitors and you present a signature strawberry creation.

**Status:** design stage — no build yet. Design is happening on the `design-lab` branch; `DESIGN.md` is the working design doc.

## The brief
- Warm, dreamy, cheerful, deliberately cute — pastel pinks, strawberry reds, cream, soft greens; a nostalgic 2000s/early-2010s browser-game/CD-ROM feel.
- The farm is the progress bar: every upgrade visibly transforms it.
- Real strawberry varieties; real baking with gluten-free as a first-class citizen; real recipes unlocked after the win.
- A canon pet pig. Failed bakes go to the pig, who considers this an excellent outcome.
