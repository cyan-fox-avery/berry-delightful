# Vankleek Hill Fair atmosphere reference (2001–2009)

**For Roman — Berry Delightful / Starvale Festival art direction**  
**Assembled:** 2026-10-11 · **Status:** Avery's childhood memories are firsthand creative reference; newspaper details and photos are supporting research. **No new festival minigames, scenes, or ride interactions approved.** The fictional Starvale festival is still on September 28, not the real Vankleek Hill Fair's August dates.

## The goal: atmosphere, not reenactment

Avery wants **the atmosphere of the Vankleek Hill Fair as he and Fiona experienced it during 2001–2009**, not a hunt for pictures of specific relatives, not a historically exact fairground model, and not a literal Vankleek Hill Fair recreation. Draw an **original small-town Eastern Ontario agricultural fair**: the carnival and the animal/exhibit areas both matter. The fair should be busy, a little loud, cheerful, slightly weathered and handmade, with bright midway lights over grass and gravel, while the game remains gentle, painterly, and player-paced. It's an agricultural community event with a midway, **not an amusement park with a farm theme**.

### Avery's confirmed firsthand ride memories (highest-priority anchors)

| Remembered ride | What Avery remembers | Visual / atmospheric use |
|---|---|---|
| **Scrambler** | A perennial favourite; returned year after year. | Recognizable radiating arms / whirling pods; classic midway motion and visual energy. |
| **Spinning strawberries** | Another perennial favourite. | Strawberry-shaped spinning kiddie-ride cars; especially apt as an affectionate *Berry Delightful* nod. |
| **Dragon** | A little/baby dragon rollercoaster. | Low, winding track and cheerful painted dragon train; small-town, family-friendly scale. |
| **Musical Ride** | One particular year's favourite: a ride that went around to music. The *remembered name* is “Musical Ride.” | A lively circular ride with music and motion. **Exact make/model unverified; do not rename it “Music Express” as a fact** or reproduce brand art based on a guess. |
| **Zipper** | The famous “rat cages” Avery and Fiona never quite dared ride: a vertically looping/rotating ride with independently tumbling cages. Avery positively identified it as **the Zipper**. | One unmistakably tall, thrilling, intimidating ride silhouette — the “maybe next year” experience, not a playable challenge. |
| **Funhouse** | **There was always a funhouse.** | Bold painted facade, slightly cheeky artwork, walk-through feel, possibly visible at the midway edge. More texture and nostalgia than a new destination. |

These are **memories across multiple years**, not a claim that every single ride appeared together on a specific fair day. The Musicial Ride/Musical Ride was especially memorable *one year*, not necessarily annual; the Scrambler, spinning strawberries, dragon and funhouse were repeat fixtures from Avery's recollection. Do not substitute Rock-O-Plane for the Zipper: a later photo album names Rock-O-Plane, but that does not negate Avery's Zipper memory.

### Visual framing for the existing game scenes

- **Foreground / strawberry festival itself:** Starvale's strawberry booth, handmade signs and bunting, baked goods, families, produce displays, neighbours milling about, slightly uneven grass, gravel paths, picnic tables, practical fencing.
- **Middle distance / agricultural fair identity:** exhibit hall or show building, 4-H / livestock signage, produce and homecraft ribbons, glimpses of animal areas and tractors where they fit the sightline. References need not imply every exhibit is explorable.
- **Background / midway:** glimpses of a **Zipper silhouette**, a **funhouse facade**, part of the **Scrambler**, a small **dragon coaster** or bright **strawberry cars**. Composition should select a few, **not cram all six into a single painting**. Concessions and a wheel ride are supporting references from actual period-adjacent photos.
- **Sound in the distance:** people, a distant PA, ride motors, laughter, snippets of fair noise, and the already decided **instrumental Irish fiddle music** at the festival. The “Musical Ride” memory suggests music matters at the midway, but does **not** authorize another permanent score/music system; check recording rights if used.
- **Emotional range:** safe/familiar rides, one year's absolutely favourite musical ride, and the awe of the Zipper they never quite rode. The **fear of the big ride is a childhood observation**, not something the player must overcome or narrate.
- **Scope protection:** the festival remains the previously agreed **walk, talk, serve, enjoy, return home** sequence through existing static scenes. Any rides visible or named are **non-interactive atmosphere** unless Avery later specifically expands scope. No playable Scrambler, Zipper or funhouse minigame.

### Small art-direction handoff

**If Roman remembers only one thing:** build the nostalgic *scale* of an early-2000s Eastern Ontario fair. From Starvale's strawberry table, you can sense the exhibit hall, hear music, smell the concessions, and see a mixture of sweet kiddie rides and the terrifying tall Zipper in the background. A funhouse was always there. **It should feel like a place Avery and Fiona actually went, without turning the little strawberry game into a carnival simulator.**
