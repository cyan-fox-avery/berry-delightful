# Design Lab — Berry Delightful

The permanent shared design thread for this game (Avery + Muse + ChatGPT) — same pattern as Fox & Found's design lab.

## Ground rules
- This branch never merges into main. Finished work graduates via its own PR.
- Speculative ideas are labeled speculative.
- Fiona-first: every decision serves the brief — a small playable love letter to Fiona.
- The design pillars hold: cozy and finite; the farm is the progress bar; real strawberry varieties; baking is the heart (gluten-free is first-class); light learning, never homework; the festival is the win.
- Placeholder names and art are fine — nothing here is final until Avery says so.

## How to use this thread
- Design discussion happens in the PR comments.
- `DESIGN.md` (repo root) is the working design doc — it grows as decisions get made.
- When a piece of design is settled, it can graduate to its own PR per the ground rules above.
